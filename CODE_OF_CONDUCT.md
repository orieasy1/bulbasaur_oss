# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

bulbasaur_oss is built by a small student team. We want every member to be able to ask questions,
disagree, and make mistakes without fear. We commit to keeping our issues, pull requests, reviews,
and team chat respectful and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with members who are learning a new tool or language.
- Review the code, not the person. Explain the reason behind a request and suggest an alternative where possible.
- Accept feedback with an open mind. If you disagree, explain why with technical reasons.
- Respond to review requests and mentions within the agreed time (24 hours), or say when you will.
- Share work fairly. Do not take credit for others' work, and disclose AI assistance honestly.
- Keep commitments. If you cannot finish an assigned task, tell the team early.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, mocking, or personal attacks, in comments or in chat.
- Harassment or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Ignoring review comments or repeatedly not responding to the team.
- Rewriting, deleting, or force-pushing over someone else's work without agreement.
- Presenting AI-generated or copied work as your own unaided work, or fabricating reviews or test results.
- Sharing other members' personal information.

## Raising a concern

1. If you feel comfortable, talk directly with the person involved first. Many problems are simple misunderstandings.
2. If that does not resolve it, or you prefer not to, contact the team maintainer privately:
   **<maintainer name>, <email or contact>**.
3. If the concern involves the maintainer, or is not resolved within the team, contact the course instructor
   through the official course channel.

Concerns are handled privately. No one will be treated unfairly for raising a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests,
reviews, the project board, team chat, and team meetings.
